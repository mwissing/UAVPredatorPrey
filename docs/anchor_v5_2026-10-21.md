# Anchor v5 (2026-10-21): symmetric reference pair

Anchor v5 is the reference predator-prey pair for the 10-pillar task with symmetric perception: pillar-relation
branches in both actors and identity-aware relation branches in both critics, trained with discount 0.995. It
passed seven of eight pre-registered gate criteria. The user accepted the miss of the forgetting criterion 3b on
2026-10-21 ("ok do as recommended"). It replaces anchor v4 as the current reference pair; anchor v4 and the graft
predator remain frozen.

- **Read-only bundle:** `.pretrained_checkpoints/anchor_v5_2026-10-21/` (gitignored). It holds README,
  MANIFEST.json, SHA256SUMS.txt, and provenance: the experiment, all evaluations, and the last joint and rehearsal
  phases' parameters.
- **Checkpoint:** `current/agent_14400.pt`, sha256 `cc17264a...`. Evaluating the bundled copy reproduced the final
  own-matchup evaluation bit-exactly.
- **Loading needs both masks and all three relation flags:**
  - `+agent.models.policy.prey_mask_inactive_predators=true`
  - `+agent.models.policy.predator_mask_inactive_teammates=true`
  - `+agent.models.policy.predator_obstacle_relation=true`
  - `+agent.models.value.obstacle_relation=true`
  - `+agent.models.policy.prey_obstacle_relation=true`

## How it was made

1. **Relation graft and continuation** (2026-10-17/18): the predators broke the waiting game against the frozen prey
   (c5). A matched control showed the graft is the cause, and it replicated at seed 71.
2. **Symmetric graft self-play** (`experiments/2026-10-19_symmetric_graft_selfplay/`): from c5's pair, the prey got
   its relation branch; 18,000 updates. The game turned into a close pillar dance, and the prey forgot the old
   obstacle-blind predator.
3. **Dance test arm H** (`experiments/2026-10-20_dance_coordination_test/`): discount 0.995 made the predators safer
   and better than a matched control (+15.5).
4. **Consolidation** (`experiments/2026-10-20_consolidation_selfplay/`): from arm H's pair, five cycles of joint self-play
   (3000 updates, a third of the envs with ghosts) and prey rehearsal (600 updates, half of the envs against fixed old
   predators), discount 0.995, 18,000 updates.
   - Per-env pool opponents get their raw states since 5a2fa0c.

## Evaluation

Deterministic, masked, 1024 episodes, seed 42. The fixed opponents are the obstacle-blind anchor v3 pair.

## Limits

- **Criterion 3b was missed and accepted.** The obstacle-blind anchor v3 predator catches this prey in 22-27% of the
  episodes, against a bar of 21.7% (anchor v4's prey: 14.7%).
  - Rehearsal brought it down from 31.6%, and the trend was still falling.
  - The weakness is against an opponent from before the pillar era.
- **The time-outs (0.104) are evasion under close pursuit, not hiding.** The waiting game is gone by its mechanism,
  but time-outs alone no longer show that.
- **Coordination is not solved.** Pincers stay rare (0.10 of the frames in which the prey orbits its pillar).
- **One seed per training phase.**
